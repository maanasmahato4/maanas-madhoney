Our Story & Heritage

For centuries, the indigenous Gurung people of Nepal have braved sheer cliff faces to harvest Apis laboriosa (giant Himalayan honey bee) honey. Known locally as "Mad Honey," this rare elixir is renowned worldwide for its medicinal benefits, subtle psychoactive properties, and deep historical value.

Our mission is to support local hunting communities through sustainable harvesting practices while delivering authentic, laboratory-tested Nepali Mad Honey directly to conscious consumers globally.
